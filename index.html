Why Tadarak Was Made

Tadarak (تدارك) embodies the imperative to catch up, rectify, and fulfill duties before time elapses. Many believers face uncertainties regarding years of missed prayers.

Tadarak serves as a structured digital ecosystem combining Hanafi-compliant calculation engines, daily micro-logging, and accountability systems to help users methodically fulfill their obligations.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are missed prayers calculated?

Calculations start from puberty (age 12) to your current age based on your submitted Date of Birth.

How do Streak Freezes work?

If you miss a full-day log, your current streak freezes for the next day instead of immediately resetting. Log all six prayer slots during that grace day to unfreeze and continue; otherwise the streak resets.

Can I use my own Qadha estimate?

Yes. On Home, enter your own total missed prayer-unit estimate and select “Use my estimate.” The total obligation uses your figure and completed logs are subtracted for the remaining count; clear the override to return to the age-based calculator.

What counts as a prayer unit in Tadarak?

The calculator counts six daily slots: five Fard prayers plus Witr as a separate slot. Each slot is one unit, not the number of traditional Rak'ahs within that prayer.

How can I stay consistent and protect my progress?

Choose a manageable daily pace, log each prayer as you complete it, and export a backup from Progress & Certificate from time to time. If enabled, Tariq checks for unlogged prayers at 12:00 noon Pakistan time. Browser reminders require Tadarak to remain open; they cannot run while the browser is closed.
