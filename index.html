Why Tadarak Was Made

Tadarak (تدارك) stems from the Arabic imperative to rectifying, catching up, or making amends before time elapses. Many believers face uncertainties regarding exact counts of unperformed prayers across years.

Tadarak was engineered as an all-in-one digital ecosystem combining Hanafi-compliant calculation engines, daily micro-logging, and social accountability mechanics to turn daunting Qadha goals into disciplined, achievable daily habits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are missed prayers calculated?

Calculations start from the age of puberty (defaulted to 12 years) up to your current age, adjusting for daily logged fulfillments.

How do Streak Freezes work?

If you miss logging for a single day, a Streak Freeze is automatically consumed from your profile balance to protect your consecutive streak count.
