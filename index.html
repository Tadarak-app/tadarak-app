Why Tadarak Was Made

Tadarak (تدارك) embodies the imperative to catch up, rectify, and fulfill duties before time elapses. Many believers face uncertainties regarding years of missed prayers.

Tadarak serves as a structured digital ecosystem combining Hanafi-compliant calculation engines, daily micro-logging, and accountability systems to help users methodically fulfill their obligations.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are missed prayers calculated?

Calculations start from puberty (age 12) to your current age based on your submitted Date of Birth.

How do Streak Freezes work?

If you miss logging for a day, a Streak Freeze is used automatically from your balance to protect your consecutive streak.
